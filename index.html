How to load Little nightmares school into Minecraft

  • Step 1: Download the LN school
  • Step 2: Setup Curseforge so you are set to download Minecraft mods
  • Step 3: In the Minecraft mods, find: Huge Structure Blocks [Fabric & Forge]
  • Step 4: An popup opens up. This will be your profile. Which means you pack of mods in that particular minecraft version. In the bottom left, press "+ New Profile". Pick a name, and press "Create"
  • Step 5: Your "modpack" now shows up in the tab "My Modpacks". Hover over it, and press the appearing button, "Play". This will open up the Minecraft launcher
  • Step 6: In the Minecraft launcher, press "Play" at the top, to start the game. This will show a popup. You can read it, but in 99% of cases, you can safely ignore this
  • Step 7: Once in Minecraft, press "Singleplayer". And create a world. For this example, i recommend an "Superflat" world
  • Step 8: Go to main menu. Go back to your worlds, and press on your world, but don't join it.
  • Step 9: Press the "Edit" button on the bottom
  • Step 10: Press "Open World" folder.
  • Step 11: Once you have your world folder open, close minecraft.
  • Step 12: Make the folder so these folders exist in your world folder: "\generated\minecraft\structure\"
  • Step 13: Move the downloaded lnschool.nbt to this folder
  • Step 14: Open Minecraft again, and join your world
  • Step 15: In you Creative tab, 2 new buttons appear at the top. Press on 1 to go to the next page. There appears a new tab, with a purple block.
  • Step 16: Grab the first Block, named: Structure Block
  • Step 17: Find an open spot without generated structures. Break 2 dirt block below you, and place the block.
  • Step 18: Right click on the block. Now a whole bunch of stuff appears. In the field "Structure Name" type "lnschool".
  • Step 19: Press the button "LOAD", in the bottom right, not the bottom left. This will show a giant box, and the text: Structure 'minecraft:lnschool' position prepared.
  • Step 20: If you are happy with the place, press "LOAD" again. Now the school will appear.
  • Step 21: Destroy the block.
  • Step 22: Have fun. All feedback is welcome
